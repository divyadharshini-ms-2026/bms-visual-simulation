How This Simulation Works

  • Voltage and current are adjustable inputs.
  • Electrical power is calculated using P = V × I.
  • The BMS checks voltage, current and temperature limits.
  • Fault conditions trigger a warning message.
  • State of Charge (SOC) is displayed as a percentage.
